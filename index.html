Recovery shares — save these now

Any two of the three open the file with no passphrase and no security key. One alone reveals nothing. They are shown once and are not stored anywhere: if you lose your passphrase or your key and have not kept at least two shares, the data is gone permanently.

Keep them in three places that cannot be destroyed together.

Check a single share

Confirms a share was transcribed correctly and belongs to the file above. It cannot confirm the share is cryptographically valid — one share carries no information about the secret, which is the point of splitting it.

Hides encrypted data in the mid-frequency DCT coefficients of an image, on JPEG's own 8×8 grid — so it survives being screenshotted, re-saved and recompressed. Measured to hold through JPEG quality 20. It does not survive cropping that shifts the grid.

Lower is less visible but breaks up sooner under compression. Higher survives more but shows as faint texture.
